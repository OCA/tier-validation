Once installed, this module will automatically create a log entry every
time a user approves or rejects a tier review on behalf of another user.

Administrators with the "Delegation Administrator" role can access this
log by navigating to:

**Settings \> Users & Companies \> Delegation Log**

The log provides a clear, searchable, and read-only history of:

- Who the original reviewer (Delegator) was.
- Who performed the action (Replacer).
- What the action was (Approved/Rejected).
- The date and time of the action.
- A direct link to the document that was reviewed.
